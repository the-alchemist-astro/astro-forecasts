## How to read the times (for the bot)
- All times are ISO 8601 in UTC (the Z suffix). Dubai time (UTC+4) is next to them for quick reading only. The bot should convert UTC times to each person's timezone.
- Day headings are Dubai days (00:00 to 24:00 Dubai). A time followed by "(28/11)" falls on another day.
- Computed geocentrically with Swiss Ephemeris. Nakshatras are sidereal (Lahiri). Signs and aspects between planets are tropical (Western). Moon timings can shift by one to two hours depending on the person's location, so times are guidance, not tied to one city.
- "Strong window" for an aspect = the period when it is within 1 degree of exact. This is my own choice, not a fixed rule.
- Nakshatra strength follows the standard classical muhurta classification. Fixed (Dhruva): Rohini, Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada = foundations, contracts, long-term starts. Movable (Chara): Punarvasu, Swati, Shravana, Dhanishta, Shatabhisha = travel, movement, trade. Soft (Mridu): Mrigashira, Chitra, Anuradha, Revati = art, love, friendship. Light (Kshipra): Ashwini, Pushya, Hasta = quick starts, learning, healing, trade. Sharp (Tikshna): Ardra, Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, Mula = not for auspicious starts. Fierce (Ugra): Bharani, Magha, Purva Phalguni, Purva Ashadha, Purva Bhadrapada = avoid gentle starts, suited to confrontation and authority. Mixed: Krittika, Vishakha.
