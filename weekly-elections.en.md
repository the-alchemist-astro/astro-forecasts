## How to read the times (for the bot)
- All times are ISO 8601 in UTC (ending in Z). Dubai time (UTC+4) is shown next to them for quick reading only. The bot should convert UTC times to each person's own timezone.
- The day in each heading is the Dubai day (00:00 to 24:00 Dubai). A time written with "(28/11)" next to an hour means it falls on another day.
- Calculation is geocentric with Swiss Ephemeris. Nakshatras use the sidereal zodiac (Lahiri). Signs and aspects between planets use the tropical (Western) zodiac. Moon times can shift by one to two hours depending on a person's location on Earth, so the times are indicative and not tied to a specific city.
- "Strong window" for an aspect = the period when the aspect is within 1 degree of exact. This is an estimate I chose, not a fixed rule.
- Nakshatra quality reference (traditional muhurta classification): Fixed (Dhruva): Rohini, Uttara Phalguni, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada = foundations, contracts, long starts. Movable (Chara): Punarvasu, Swati, Shravana, Dhanishta, Shatabhisha = travel, movement, trade. Soft (Mridu): Mrigashira, Chitra, Anuradha, Revati = art, love, friendship. Swift (Kshipra): Ashwini, Pushya, Hasta = quick starts, learning, healing, trade. Sharp (Tikshna): Ardra, Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, Mula = not suited to auspicious beginnings. Fierce (Ugra): Bharani, Magha, Purva Phalguni, Purva Ashadha, Purva Bhadrapada = avoid gentle starts; suited to confrontation and authority. Mixed: Krittika, Vishakha.
